Features

Everything You Need for Better Housing

Our platform combines institutional verification with smart matching to ensure you find the perfect roommate from your campus community.

🛡
Institutional Verification
Only verified students from your institution can join, eliminating stranger danger and ensuring a safe community.
📈
Smart Matching Algorithm
Our weighted scoring system analyzes routines, social habits, and interests to find your most compatible roommate.
💬
Secure Messaging
Chat with potential roommates before finalizing your choice. Get to know each other in a safe environment.
🏢
Room Inventory
Browse available rooms in your campus hostels with detailed amenity listings and pricing information.
👥
Compatibility Survey
Complete a lifestyle questionnaire covering sleep habits, social preferences, and interests for accurate matching.
🏆
Mutual Confirmation
Both students must accept the match before confirming, ensuring everyone is comfortable with the arrangement.
How It Works

Three Simple Steps

Getting matched with the perfect roommate has never been easier.

01
